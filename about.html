About Gridwork Systems

Spreadsheet systems for small businesses. Every method is explained in our guides, and two of the files are free with no signup.

Gridwork Systems started in November 2026. That is worth saying plainly, because it decides what we can and cannot claim. We have no customer stories yet and no years of trading to point to. What we do have is a set of spreadsheet systems, the method behind each one written out in full in our guides, and two of the files free to download with no signup.

The systems follow an order that makes sense for a small business. A 12-month cashflow forecast shows the tight months coming. An invoice register tells you who to call on Monday morning. Job costing, a per-client payment summary and a quote calculator then link the first two together.

What we believe

  • You should see every number. At small scale, visibility beats automation. A spreadsheet you understand outperforms software you don't.
  • You should own your tools. Your bookkeeping shouldn't be rented month-to-month, hostage to a price hike. Files on your machine, forever.
  • Free should mean free. The 12-month forecaster is free with no signup — because we'd rather earn trust than harvest emails.
  • Honesty compounds. Our guides tell you when to stop using spreadsheets and buy real accounting software. That honesty is the marketing.

How the systems are made

Each template is an ordinary Excel workbook: every formula is visible, there are no macros and no add-ons, and the file opens in Google Sheets. Each one is built by script and recalculated before it goes into the store, so that a broken formula is caught rather than sold, and the same check runs again whenever a file changes. The files and the guides on this site are produced with AI assistance, working for Gridwork Systems. The guide for each system explains the method in full, so you can build the sheet yourself, or judge ours before you pay for it. What we have not done is run a business on these files for years. If you do, tell us what breaks.

Where the name comes from

A grid — the spreadsheet. Work — the business. Gridwork: the quiet infrastructure under a business that runs well. That is what we sell.
