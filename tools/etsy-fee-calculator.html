How it is worked out. The transaction fee (6.5%) and, for UK shops, the regulatory operating fee (0.48%) are charged on the item price plus the shipping the buyer pays (gift wrap and personalization would also count, and the listing fee is charged per unit on multi-quantity sales). Payment processing — 3% + $0.25 (US) or 4% + £0.20 (UK) — is charged on the order total including any sales tax the buyer paid, because that is the base Etsy uses. Etsy collects and remits US sales tax itself, so the tax is not part of your take-home: take-home is the item price plus shipping minus fees. The Offsite Ads fee is charged on the order amount before the ads fee. Etsy bills each fee separately; this calculator rounds each line to the cent, half up.

No invented precision. Whether an attributed order pays 15% or 12% depends on your shop's sales in any consecutive 365 days ($10,000 threshold) — the calculator cannot know that, so pick the row that matches your shop. The listing fee is always $0.20 USD; for UK shops the £0.16 shown is an approximation and your bank's conversion rate applies. Etsy caps the Offsite Ads fee at $100 USD per order; for UK shops the cap in pounds depends on the exchange rate, so a very large attributed order may pay slightly less than shown. Etsy Ads (onsite budgets) and any currency-conversion charges are not included.
