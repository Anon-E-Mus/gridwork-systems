Free Food Safety Log (UK)

Free — no signup, no email

A month of daily temperature, probe and cleaning records for a UK food business. Free, no signup.

A one-page-a-day food safety log for a small UK food business: fridge, freezer and hot-holding temperatures, a probe reading with what was probed, cleaning, and the corrective action when something is wrong - 31 days per copy, with a Check column that names each fault and a Summary that counts the month. The limits shipped as defaults are Food Standards Agency recommendations or legal maxima, and every one carries its source and quote on the Limits tab.

Download the sheet (Excel) opens in Excel and Google Sheets

What's in it

  • How to use tab: what to record, when to record it, and what to write down when a reading is wrong
  • Limits tab: four equipment names you can rename (two fridges, a freezer and a hot cabinet by default), each limit with its source URL and verbatim quote, plus a block for Scotland, where the requirements differ
  • Daily Log: 31 days - date, four equipment temperatures, probe temperature and what was probed, cleaning Y/N, corrective action, signed
  • Check column that names each fault: which equipment is above or below its limit, no corrective action recorded, cleaning not recorded
  • Summary tab: days recorded, out-of-range counts per column, rows without corrective action, days without cleaning, last entry date
  • Plain formulas only, no macros; a reading turns red when it is outside its limit

How to use it

  • Fill the yellow cells on the Limits tab first: your four equipment names and the limits you work to
  • Record each reading at the time you take it, not from memory at the end of the day
  • The Check column names what is wrong: which equipment is outside its limit, no corrective action recorded, cleaning not recorded
  • The Summary tab counts the month: days recorded, out-of-range readings, missing corrective actions, days without cleaning

Good to know

  • The default limits are Food Standards Agency recommendations or legal maxima; the Limits tab names the source and quotes it for every number
  • The freezer limit ships blank: no official UK page gives a number, so you set your own
  • Requirements differ in Scotland - there is a block for it on the Limits tab
  • This is a record-keeping aid, not a food safety management system, and not a substitute for training or for Safer food, better business
  • Works in Excel and Google Sheets; 31 days per copy - save a fresh copy for each month
