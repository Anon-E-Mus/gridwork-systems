Free One-Job Cost Sheet

Free — no signup, no email

Cost one job before you quote it, then see whether you were right. Free, no signup.

An Excel sheet for a single job: labor at its loaded rate, materials, subcontractors and other direct costs, overhead as a percentage, then the quote and your margin. Fill the same lines in again with what the job actually cost and the sheet shows the variance, flagging anything more than 10% over.

Download the sheet (Excel) opens in Excel and Google Sheets

What's in it

  • Labor: hours x loaded rate, task by task
  • Materials: quantity x unit cost, with room for waste
  • Subcontractors and other direct costs
  • Overhead as a percentage of direct cost
  • Estimated and actual side by side, variance flagged red above 10%
  • Quote price, margin and margin % - planned against actual

How to use it

  • Before you quote: fill the yellow cells on the estimate side - hours per task, quantities, unit costs, your overhead %, the quote price
  • While the job runs: write what it actually costs in the actual columns, the day it happens
  • Afterwards: read the variance. More than 10% over the estimate is flagged red - that is the line to price differently next time
