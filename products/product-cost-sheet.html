Free Product Cost Sheet

Free — no signup, no email

What one unit really costs you, and what you earn when you sell it. Free, no signup.

An Excel sheet for one product: materials per batch with the size you buy them in, the minutes you actually work, packaging, overhead as a percentage, and the fees on each sale. It gives the cost per unit, the profit at your price, the break-even price and the price that earns your target margin.

Download the sheet (Excel) opens in Excel and Google Sheets

What's in it

  • Materials: amount used per batch, purchase size, price paid
  • Labor: minutes per stage at your hourly rate
  • Packaging per unit
  • Overhead as a percentage of materials, labor and packaging
  • Fees per sale: a percentage plus a fixed part
  • Cost per unit, profit per unit, margin, break-even price, target price

How to use it

  • At the top: your product, how many units one batch makes, your hourly rate, the price you charge
  • Materials: what goes into one batch - how much you use, how much you buy it as, what you paid
  • Labor: the minutes you are actually working; then packaging and the fees on each sale
  • Read the cost per unit, the profit at your price, the break-even price and the price that gives your target margin
