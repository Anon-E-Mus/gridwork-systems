What Etsy actually charges per sale: the 2026 fee breakdown (with a working calculator)

Listing, transaction, processing, offsite ads: every Etsy fee explained with real math — a $29.50 order loses $3.26 before your costs. Includes the easily-missed Share & Save refund.

Etsy publishes its rates but never adds them up for you, and it buries two of them three help-center pages deep. So here is the full picture of Etsy fees in 2026: every fee explained in plain English, one real order walked through to the cent, and the spreadsheet setup that works as an Etsy fee calculator. All rates current as of November 2026.

How much does Etsy take? The short answer

Three fees hit almost every US order:

1. Listing fee — $0.20. Flat, always in USD, charged when a listing publishes, again at every 4-month renewal, and again automatically each time an item sells. A multi-quantity sale pays $0.20 per unit.

2. Transaction fee — 6.5%. Of the total order amount: item price + shipping + gift wrap. It has not moved since April 11, 2022, when it went up from 5%. No increase in 2025 or 2026.

3. Payment processing — 3% + $0.25 per order (US rates). Charged on the full amount including shipping and any sales tax the buyer paid.

Stack those on a small order and you are at roughly 11% before you have paid for a single material. Then two more wait in the weeds: an Offsite Ads fee (15% or 12%) that only shows up when it triggers, and a Regulatory Operating Fee that US sellers skip but sellers in nine countries pay. Both covered below.

One real order, to the cent

Item price $24.00, shipping $5.50, order total $29.50. No gift wrap, no sales tax on this one, US shop, order did not come from an ad.

$3.26 in fees on a $29.50 order. You take home $26.24 — an effective fee rate of 11.05%, before materials, before your time. That is the number to memorize: not 6.5%, eleven-percent-ish on small orders.

(If the buyer had paid sales tax, the 3% would apply to the tax too — money you never kept anyway.)

Now the part that stings. If that order had come through an Offsite Ad at 15%, add $4.43 (15% of $29.50 = $4.425). Total fees: $7.69. Same box, same buyer, 26% gone. More on that trap in a minute.

The Offsite Ads trap

Offsite Ads is Etsy advertising your listings on Google, Facebook and elsewhere. When a buyer clicks one of those ads and buys within 30 days, you pay:

  • 15% if your shop has always made under $10,000 in sales in any consecutive 365-day period. You are auto-enrolled, but you may opt out (Settings > Offsite Ads) and re-enroll anytime.
  • 12% once your shop has made $10,000 or more in any consecutive 365-day period. At that point participation is mandatory for the life of the shop. Etsy checks monthly. There is no opt-out.

Three details that make it a trap:

1. The 30-day window. The buyer clicks your ad, thinks about it for three weeks, then buys. Fee applies. Multiple orders inside the window from one click can each be charged.

2. The fee base is the order amount before the ads fee — item + shipping + gift wrap. On our $29.50 example that is $4.43 stacked on top of the $3.26 you already paid.

3. The cap is real but high: $100 per order. At 15%, the cap arrives at $666.67 orders; at 12%, at $833.33. The cap protects furniture sellers, not sticker sellers.

The refund many sellers never claim

Share & Save: when a buyer arrives through a link you shared yourself — Instagram bio, newsletter, wherever — Etsy refunds part of the transaction fee. Normally the refund is 4% of the sale. Through January 4, 2027, the holiday boost refunds the full 6.5% transaction fee. Other fees still apply.

On our example order, that is $1.92 back for sharing your own listing link. It is the only fee Etsy actively hands back, it costs nothing, and it is easy to have never opened the tab.

If you sell from the UK

Same skeleton, different numbers:

  • Listing fee: still $0.20 USD — it is always USD, everywhere.
  • Transaction fee: 6.5%, same as the US.
  • Payment processing: 4% + £0.20 per order — a full point above the US percentage, charged on the total including shipping and tax.
  • Regulatory Operating Fee: 0.48% of item + shipping + gift wrap (it was 0.32% until June 22, 2026).

Quick comparison on a £29.50 order: £1.92 transaction + £1.38 processing (£1.18 + £0.20) + £0.14 regulatory = £3.44 in fees, plus the $0.20 listing. That runs about 11.7% — the regulatory fee and the higher processing rate are the difference from the US.

3 fee mistakes sellers make

1. Forgetting Etsy fees your shipping. Both the 6.5% and the 3% apply to what you charge for postage (and the regulatory fee includes shipping where it applies). A $5.50 shipping charge costs you about $0.52 in fees. If actual postage costs you $5.50, you are subsidizing every order. Price shipping at cost-plus-fees, or build it into the item price — "free shipping" dodges nothing; Etsy taxes it either way.

2. Treating the $0.20 listing fee as one-time. It is charged at publish, at every 4-month renewal, and per unit on multi-quantity sales. A 5-unit order carries $1.00 of listing fees alone. Fine on a $200 order. Painful on five $6 stickers.

3. Quoting "6.5%" as your fee rate. The fixed fees dominate small orders. Our $29.50 order pays an effective 11.05%. A $100 order pays 9.95%. A $10 order pays $1.40 — 14%. If your average order is under $20, Etsy's real cut is closer to 13–14% than 6.5%, and your prices need to know that.

FAQ

Does Etsy charge fees on shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee and the 3% + $0.25 processing fee both apply to shipping (and gift wrap). In the nine regulatory-fee countries, that fee also includes shipping in its base. Free shipping does not avoid this — the fees just move to the item price.

Is the regulatory operating fee charged in the US?

No. US sellers do not pay it. It applies by bank-account country: UK 0.48%, France 1.14%, Italy 0.80%, Spain 0.88%, Türkiye 1.67%, India 0.05%, Hungary 1.97%, Vietnam 1.24%, Canada 0.50% (rates effective June 22, 2026).

What's Etsy's fee on a $100 sale?

Item $100, free shipping, US shop, no ads: $0.20 listing + $6.50 transaction (6.5%) + $3.25 processing (3% + $0.25) = $9.95. You keep $90.05. If the order came through an Offsite Ad, add $15 at the 15% rate or $12 at 12% — total fees of $24.95 or $21.95.

Can I turn Offsite Ads off?

Under $10,000 in sales in any consecutive 365-day period: yes, opt out in Settings > Offsite Ads. Once your shop crosses $10,000 in any 365-day window: no. The 12% rate is mandatory for the life of the shop, capped at $100 per order.
