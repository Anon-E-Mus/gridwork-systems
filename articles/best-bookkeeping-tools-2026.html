The spreadsheet vs accounting software question, answered first

The honest 2026 answer is boring: both are right, at different volumes. A service business doing under roughly 100 transactions a month, with no payroll, loses money on accounting software: $230 to $420+ a year for automation that saves minutes. Past that line, or the day payroll starts, software earns its price and the spreadsheet becomes stubbornness. Six of the seven tools below live on the spreadsheet side of that line. One, flagged loudly, is not a bookkeeping tool at all.

Where our bias sits

We are Gridwork Systems. We sell spreadsheet templates on Whop, and four of the tools below are ours, bias declared.
