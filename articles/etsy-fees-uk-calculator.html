Etsy fees for UK sellers in 2026: what Etsy actually takes (with the fee math)

How much does Etsy take in the UK? The short answer

Four fees hit almost every UK order — one more than US sellers pay:

1. Listing fee — $0.20. Flat, and always in US dollars, wherever you sell. Charged when a listing publishes, at every four-month renewal, and again automatically each time an item sells.

2. Transaction fee — 6.5%. Of item price + shipping + gift wrap. Identical to the US rate, unchanged since April 2022.

3. Payment processing — 4% + £0.20 per order. The UK rate, a full percentage point above the US 3%. Charged on the order total including shipping and VAT where applicable.

4. Regulatory Operating Fee — 0.48%. The one US sellers never pay. Charged on item + shipping + gift wrap, on top of the transaction fee — and it went up 50% in June 2026.

Stack those on a small order and you are at roughly 12% before paying for a single material. Then Offsite Ads (15% or 12%) waits in the weeds — it only shows up when it triggers.

One real order, to the penny

Item price £24.00, shipping £5.50, order total £29.50. No gift wrap, UK shop, order did not come from an ad. Same numbers as our US fee breakdown, in pounds, so you can compare markets directly.

£3.60 in fees on a £29.50 order. You take home £25.90 — an effective rate of 12.2%, or 11.7% excluding the listing fee. That is the number to memorise: not 6.5%, twelve-percent-ish on small orders.

On the rounding, because we would rather show the arithmetic than hide it: £29.50 × 6.5% is £1.9175, billed as £1.92. £29.50 × 0.48% is £0.1416, billed as £0.14. The 4% is £1.18 exactly; add the fixed £0.20 for £1.38. The listing fee is always $0.20 USD — £0.16 is an approximation at recent rates, your bank's rate applies, so expect a few pence of drift on your statement.

Now the part that stings. Had the order come through an Offsite Ad at 15%, add £4.43 (15% of £29.50 = £4.425). Total fees: £8.03. Same box, same buyer, a touch over 27% gone.

The June 2026 increase nobody budgeted for

Announced April 2026, effective 22 June 2026: the UK Regulatory Operating Fee rose from 0.32% to 0.48% — a 50% increase, applied per order.

In practice: on our £29.50 example, the fee went from £0.09 to £0.14. Per £10,000 of item + shipping sales, it went from £32 to £48 — an extra £16 per £10,000 of revenue. Not fatal. But if you priced your shop in May 2026, your margins quietly moved in June.

VAT: where it touches your fees

Two questions UK sellers ask constantly:

Does Etsy handle VAT for me? Partly. Etsy collects VAT where the rules require it — digital services are the classic case — and collects buyer VAT in many situations. Whether you must register yourself depends on your taxable turnover, and the registration threshold is set by HMRC and changes. Check the current threshold on HMRC's own pages rather than trusting a blog post — including this one.

Do fees apply to VAT? Where a buyer pays VAT on an order, the 4% processing fee is charged on the VAT-inclusive total. You are paying a fee on tax you were only holding on HMRC's behalf — a small amount per order, but it belongs in your maths, not outside it.

The $0.20 that is never quite £0.16

The listing fee is always charged in US dollars, and the Offsite Ads cap is defined in USD too ($100 per order). The conversion happens on your bill and bank statement — your bank's rate applies, plus any currency-conversion charge your account carries. At recent rates $0.20 lands near £0.16, the figure we use throughout this piece, but it moves with the market. If you reconcile in GBP, budget a rounding line rather than a hard £0.16.

The one fee Etsy hands back

If you have never opened this tab, you are not alone: Share & Save refunds part of your transaction fee when a buyer arrives through a link you shared yourself. Through 4 January 2027 the holiday boost refunds the full 6.5% — on our example order, £1.92 back for putting your own listing link in your Instagram bio. It is the only fee Etsy actively returns.

FAQ

Does Etsy charge VAT on fees?

It depends on your situation, and we will not pretend otherwise. Etsy collects VAT where the rules require it — digital services being the common case — and whether VAT applies to the fees on your own bill depends on your registration status and the current rules. Check Etsy's fee pages and HMRC's current guidance together; thresholds change, and an out-of-date number costs real money.

What's the fee on a £100 sale in the UK?

Item £100, free shipping, UK shop, no ads: £6.50 transaction (6.5%) + £4.20 processing (4% + £0.20) + £0.48 regulatory (0.48%) = £11.18, plus the $0.20 (~£0.16) listing fee — call it £11.34 all in. You keep roughly £88.66 — about 11.3% effective. If the order came through an Offsite Ad, add £15 at the 15% rate or £12 at 12% — total fees near £26.34 or £23.34.

Is Offsite Ads mandatory in the UK?

Same rules as everywhere on Etsy. If your shop has always made under $10,000 in sales in any consecutive 365-day period, you pay 15% when it triggers but may opt out (Settings > Offsite Ads). Once your shop makes $10,000 or more in any 365-day window, the 12% rate is mandatory for the life of the shop — no opt-out, Etsy checks monthly, capped at $100 USD per order.
