Meet the Invoice & Payment Tracker

The who-owes-us-what notebook, retired: a $19 register that ages your invoices, works out how long customers take to pay, and hands you the Monday call list.

Product release2 min readGridwork Systems · Nov 2026

The "who owes us what" notebook, retired. A $19 register that ages your invoices, works out how long customers take to pay, and hands you the Monday call list.

Every business that invoices has a second, unpaid job: remembering who owes what, how late, and who needs chasing. That job usually gets run from memory and a notebook. Memory doesn't scale, and neither does the queasy Monday-morning reconstruction.

What it does

Log each invoice once — client, amount, issue date, due date — and the tracker does the rest:

  • Totals, tax, due dates and balances calculated on every line, with invoice numbers assigned in order
  • Aging by days late computed automatically: not yet due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90 and 90+ days, with days sales outstanding (DSO)
  • A Monday call list on the dashboard: the open invoices, oldest due date first
  • A printable invoice for any invoice number you pick
  • Overdue flags on every unpaid line past its due date

The Monday routine it enables

Open the dashboard and read the Monday call list, oldest due date first. Anyone more than thirty days late gets a call — not an email, a call. Anyone past sixty gets a decision made on purpose: prepayment, pause, or a payment plan with dates. Fifteen minutes, and your receivables stop being a mood and start being a system.
